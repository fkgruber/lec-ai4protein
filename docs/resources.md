## Key Papers

### Transformers and Attention
1. Vaswani et al. (2017). ["Attention Is All You Need"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
2. Devlin et al. (2019). ["BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805)

### Graph Neural Networks
3. Kipf & Welling (2017). ["Semi-Supervised Classification with Graph Convolutional Networks"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907)
4. Veličković et al. (2018). ["Graph Attention Networks"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10903)
5. Gilmer et al. (2017). ["Neural Message Passing for Quantum Chemistry"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.01212)

### Generative Models
6. Kingma & Welling (2014). ["Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6114)
7. Ho et al. (2020). ["Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239)

### Protein Language Models
8. Rives et al. (2021). ["Biological structure and function emerge from scaling unsupervised learning"](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2016239118) (ESM-1b)
9. Lin et al. (2023). ["Evolutionary-scale prediction of atomic-level protein structure with a language model"](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ade2574) (ESM-2/ESMFold)

### Structure Prediction
10. Jumper et al. (2021). ["Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold"](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03819-2)
11. Baek et al. (2021). ["Accurate prediction of protein structures and interactions using RoseTTAFold"](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abj8754)

### Protein Design
12. Dauparas et al. (2022). ["Robust deep learning–based protein sequence design using ProteinMPNN"](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.add2187)
13. Watson et al. (2023). ["De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion"](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06415-8)
14. Ingraham et al. (2023). ["Illuminating protein space with a programmable generative model"](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06728-8) (Chroma)

## Databases and Datasets

### Sequence Databases
- [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/) - Protein sequences and annotations
- [Pfam](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/pfam/) - Protein families
- [UniRef](https://www.uniprot.org/help/uniref) - Clustered sequences

### Structure Databases
- [RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/) - Experimental structures
- [AlphaFold Database](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/) - Predicted structures
- [CATH](https://www.cathdb.info/) - Protein structure classification
- [SCOPe](https://scop.berkeley.edu/) - Structural classification

### Benchmark Datasets
- [CASP](https://predictioncenter.org/) - Structure prediction benchmarks
- [TAPE](https://github.com/songlab-cal/tape) - Tasks Assessing Protein Embeddings
- [ProteinGym](https://proteingym.org/) - Fitness prediction benchmarks
